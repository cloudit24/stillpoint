Stillpoint is a calm, minimal home screen. Black background, text first, no clutter.

<b>Home</b>
<ul>
<li>Clock in six styles, including retro flip and LCD</li>
<li>Optional animated weather, gold price in your currency, live network speed and RAM</li>
<li>Today's screen time and (optionally) today's calendar</li>
<li>Your most used or pinned apps, as text or icons only, in the size you like</li>
<li>Customizable gestures: swipe left, right, up, down and double-tap, plus two bottom shortcuts</li>
</ul>

<b>App list</b>
<ul>
<li>Tabs: Most used, Recent, All, Favorites</li>
<li>Alphabet bar for fast scrolling</li>
<li>Favorites with your own folders</li>
<li>Search that opens the first match on Enter</li>
</ul>

<b>Also</b>
<ul>
<li>Widget page with your task list</li>
<li>Focus mode that blocks chosen apps for a set time</li>
<li>Hide apps, work profile support</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
No analytics, no ads, no Google Play Services. The app goes online only for features you switch on (weather from Open-Meteo, gold price, currency rates), and never sends anything about you.
