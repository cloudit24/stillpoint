Stillpoint Launcher is a calm, minimal home screen. Black background, text first, one important thing at a time.

<b>Home</b>
<ul>
<li>A headline that flips like a live tile: the time, screen time, weather, Hijri and Tamil dates</li>
<li>A terminal display above your apps that tells you what matters now: iqama, missed calls, battery, focus, your next event. Terminal, Retro LCD or a quiet line</li>
<li>A ring beside it: countdown to the next prayer, battery, or time left today</li>
<li>Under it, your choice of prayer times, the next calendar event, or the day and battery</li>
<li>Your most used or pinned apps, as text or icons</li>
<li>Edge light: a line on the screen edge that fills towards the next prayer. Made for curved screens, with styles for flat ones</li>
<li>Gestures for every swipe and double-tap, a search button, accent colours and grey icons</li>
</ul>

<b>Islamic prayer (optional)</b>
<ul>
<li>Prayer times calculated on the phone, with ten methods and Hanafi Asr</li>
<li>Prayer and iqama alerts, with iqama times you set per prayer</li>
<li>A full-screen alert at the adhan and iqama, with Remind in 5 min only while the iqama is far enough away</li>
<li>Friday in the UAE: the alert comes at Dhuhr, with a countdown to Jumu'ah</li>
<li>Qibla compass that ticks as you turn and taps when you face the Qibla</li>
<li>Lock screen countdown to the next prayer</li>
</ul>

<b>Focus and wellbeing</b>
<ul>
<li>Focus profiles: Work, Prayer, Sleep, Family or your own, each with its apps and an optional daily schedule</li>
<li>Always-allowed apps for family and work emergencies</li>
<li>A short pause before apps that are hard to put down, daily limits, a screen-time goal and a calm home</li>
<li>Sticky notes on the Shelf, and your calendar from the phone or a calendar link (.ics)</li>
</ul>

<b>Also</b>
<ul>
<li>App list with pivot tabs, alphabet bar, favorites and folders</li>
<li>Widget page, plus Stillpoint widgets for any launcher</li>
<li>Data usage per app, hide apps, work profile support</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
No analytics, no ads, no accounts, no Google Play Services. Settings never leave the phone. The app goes online only for features you switch on, and never sends anything about you.
